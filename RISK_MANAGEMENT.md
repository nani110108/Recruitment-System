# Recruitment System
# Risk Management Document

## Aim

To identify, analyze and manage the risks
associated with the Recruitment System.

## Risk Identification

| Risk ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Risk Level |
|---------|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| R1 | Loss of candidate data | Medium | High | High |
| R2 | Unauthorized access | Medium | High | High |
| R3 | Server failure | Medium | High | High |
| R4 | Changes in requirements | High | Medium | High |
| R5 | Delay in development | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| R6 | Resume upload failure | Medium | Medium | Medium |
| R7 | Database corruption | Low | High | Medium |
| R8 | Incorrect application status | Medium | High | High |
| R9 | Integration failure | Medium | High | High |
| R10 | Insufficient testing | Medium | High | High |

## Risk Management Strategies

1. Maintain regular database backups.
2. Use secure login and password hashing.
3. Perform regular system maintenance.
4. Maintain proper project documentation.
5. Follow a development schedule.
6. Validate uploaded resume files.
7. Test database operations regularly.
8. Validate application status updates.
9. Perform integration testing.
10. Execute all planned test cases.

## Result

The Risk Management document for the
Recruitment System was prepared successfully.
